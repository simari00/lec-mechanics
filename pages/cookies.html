1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data a website stores in your browser. They help websites remember your preferences and keep you signed in.

2. Cookies we use

CookieTypePurpose
lecCookieConsent Strictly necessary Stores YOUR cookie choice (accept / reject / custom) so we do not ask you on every visit. Lasts 1 year. You can change or erase it any time using the controls on this page.
lec_session Strictly necessary Keeps administrators signed in to the admin panel. It is a secure, HTTP-only session cookie and is deleted when you sign out. Regular visitors never receive this cookie.
lecTheme (local storage) Functional Remembers whether you chose light or dark mode on the website. Only stored after you allow functional cookies — if you reject, your theme choice applies to the current visit only and is erased.

3. What we do NOT use

We do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers or social-media pixels. Nothing on this site follows you around the internet.

4. Managing cookies

On your first visit we show a cookie banner: Accept all, Reject optional, or Preferences to choose category by category. Your choice is a real cookie on your device and the site obeys it.

Changed your mind? Use the controls in "Your current cookie choices" at the top of this page — allow, reject, or reopen the banner. Withdrawing consent erases the functional data immediately and the banner returns on your next visit.

You can also clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time (usually under Privacy & Security). Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent administrators from signing in, but the public website will keep working normally.
