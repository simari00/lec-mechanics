Cookies Preferences

Last updated: 17 September 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data a website stores in your browser. They help websites remember your preferences and keep you signed in.

2. Cookies we use

CookieTypePurpose
lec_session Strictly necessary Keeps administrators signed in to the admin panel. It is a secure, HTTP-only session cookie and is deleted when you sign out. Regular visitors never receive this cookie.
lecTheme (local storage) Functional Remembers whether you chose light or dark mode on the website.

3. What we do NOT use

We do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers or social-media pixels. Nothing on this site follows you around the internet.

4. Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time (usually under Privacy & Security). Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent administrators from signing in, but the public website will keep working normally. Your theme preference is stored locally and you can clear it any time via your browser's "clear site data" option.
