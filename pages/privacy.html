Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

1. Who we are

LEC Mechanics ("we", "us") is an automotive repair garage in Harare, Zimbabwe. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and what we do with it.

2. What we collect

  • Service requests: your name, phone number, email (optional), vehicle registration, and the details of your request.
  • Apprenticeship applications: your name, surname, age, phone number, O/A Level qualifications, technical subjects and driver's licence status.
  • Business records: if you become a customer, we keep records of the work done on your vehicle and the invoices issued.
  • Basic technical data: your browser sends standard information (such as IP address) to any website you visit; we do not use it to profile you.

3. How we use it

We use your information only to: respond to and complete your service request, contact you about your vehicle or application, keep legally required business records, and improve our services. We do not sell your information to anyone.

4. Tracking codes

Each request gets a tracking code so you can follow its progress. Anyone who knows the code can view that request's status, so keep it private like a password.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored in a secured cloud database. Admin accounts are protected with hashed passwords, login attempt limits and approval by the master administrator. We keep business records for as long as the law requires, then delete them.

6. Sharing

We share your information only with service providers that help us operate (for example our website hosting and database provider) and, where necessary, with insurers or authorities in connection with work on your vehicle.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to correct or delete your personal information at any time. Call 0771 232 171 or email us and we will action reasonable requests promptly.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page with a new "last updated" date.
